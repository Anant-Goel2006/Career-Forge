# Product Requirements Document — CareerForge AI

## Problem
Candidates waste time understanding job descriptions, finding resume weaknesses, tailoring applications, formatting one-page resumes, and preparing outreach. CareerForge turns that into one guided workflow.

## Target users
Students, fresh graduates, early-career professionals, career switchers, and technology/data candidates.

## MVP
1. PDF/DOCX upload.
2. Secure resume parsing and editable profile.
3. Resume health audit.
4. Job description paste/upload.
5. Required/preferred requirement extraction.
6. Transparent resume-job relevance analysis.
7. Skill-gap analysis.
8. Evidence-backed one-page resume tailoring.
9. Professional DOCX/PDF export.
10. Versioning and application history.

## Resume Health
Detect grammar/wording problems, weak bullets, repetition, poor section hierarchy, inconsistent dates, missing contact data, formatting risks, vague claims, lack of genuine quantification, and job-specific relevance gaps.

## Tailoring
Use role, employment type, experience level and job requirements to reorder and rewrite genuine evidence. Use STAR principles without fabricating results. Never add unsupported skills or metrics.

## Score
Provide transparent sub-scores: required-skill coverage, preferred-skill coverage, evidence strength, role fit, experience fit, education fit, location fit, keyword alignment, formatting/readiness. Never promise universal ATS compatibility or guaranteed 99/100.

## Resume output
FAANG-inspired technical one-page template plus Analytics, Data Science and Business variants. Proper margins, spacing, line height, alignment, section hierarchy, hyperlinks, editable DOCX and PDF.

## Outreach — post-MVP
Find relevant public professional contacts using approved sources, show source/confidence, and generate LinkedIn/email/referral drafts. User must review before sending.

## MVP non-goals
No automatic applications, bulk messaging, private-data scraping, platform bypasses, fabricated achievements, autonomous external communication, or training on resumes without explicit permission.

## Success criteria
High parsing accuracy on internal fixtures, zero unsupported claims in validation tests, reliable one-page export, strong accessibility, no critical/high security issues before production.
