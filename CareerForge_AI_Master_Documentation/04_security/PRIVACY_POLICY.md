# Privacy Policy Draft — Engineering Baseline
Not legal advice; obtain legal review before public/commercial use.

Collected: account data, resumes, job descriptions, preferences, applications, generated versions, outreach drafts and security logs.

Purpose: provide resume analysis, job matching, document generation, application tracking and service security.

AI: send only minimum necessary data to external providers; review provider retention/training settings.

Controls: view/correct/delete data; delete account; deletion should cover database, object storage, embeddings and caches where feasible.

Security: encryption in transit/at rest where supported, least privilege and audit logging.

Recruiter discovery: public professional information only; no private contact guessing.

Retention: define category-specific periods before launch.
